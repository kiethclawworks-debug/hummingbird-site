🌿 CA Native Flowers for Hummingbirds

Pair your feeders with these California native plants (far better than the state poppy for birds):

  • California Fuchsia (Epilobium canum) — Red blooms July-November, thrives in dry slopes
  • Desert Willow (Chilopsis linearis) — Pink/white May-September, Southern CA native
  • Chuparosa (Justicia californica) — Red winter blooms, desert regions
  • Scarlet Monkey Flower (Erythranthe cardinalis) — Streambanks, red/orange April-October
  • Native Salvias — Blue/purple, various species bloom year-round

📊 California Regional Guide

Coastal California (SF, LA, San Diego):

  • Anna's Hummingbirds year-round
  • Feeders: Keep up all year!
  • Best flowers: Salvias, CA fuchsia, monkey flower
  • Nectar: Anna's Formula (3:1)

Desert California (Palm Springs, Anza-Borrego):

  • Costa's + Anna's Hummingbirds year-round
  • Water sources critical (misters, fountains)
  • Best flowers: Ocotillo, chuparosa, desert willow
  • Nectar: Anna's Formula (3:1) — desert concentration

Central Valley (Sacramento, Fresno):

  • Anna's + Black-chinned in summer
  • Hot summers — change nectar every 2 days
  • Best flowers: Desert willow, buckeye, penstemon
  • Nectar: Anna's Formula (3:1) or Black-Chinned Blend (4:1)

Sierra Nevada Mountains:

  • Rufous migrants (March-April north, July-September south)
  • Anna's at lower elevations
  • Best flowers: Columbine, penstemon, Indian paintbrush
  • Nectar: Rufous Energy Mix (3:1) during migration peaks
