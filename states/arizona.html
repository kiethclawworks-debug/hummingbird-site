🌿 Arizona Native Flowers for Hummingbirds

  • Chuparosa (Justicia californica) — Red, blooms Nov-April, quintessential Sonoran desert hummingbird flower
  • Desert Willow (Chilopsis linearis) — Pink/white April-September, statewide
  • Ocotillo (Fouquieria splendens) — Red/orange after rain, March-June
  • Red Yucca (Hesperaloe parviflora) — Coral/pink March-October, extremely drought-tolerant
  • Arizona Trumpet Vine — Orange/red, mountain canyons, southeast AZ
