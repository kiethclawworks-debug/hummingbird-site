🌸 Tennessee State Flower: Iris

The Iris became Tennessee's state flower in 1933. While not all iris varieties are hummingbird favorites, several native and garden varieties pair beautifully with feeder strategies.

Best Iris Varieties for Hummingbirds in TN:

  • Blue Flag Iris (Iris virginica) — Native to TN wetlands, blooms April-May
  • Crested Iris (Iris cristata) — Shade-tolerant, early spring nectar
  • Bearded Iris hybrids — Later season (May-June), good for transition
💡 Pro Tip: Plant iris in drifts near your feeder stations. The color attracts hummingbirds to the area, and early-blooming varieties provide natural nectar before your feeders go up.

🌿 TN Native Flowers That Attract Hummingbirds

Pair your feeders with these Tennessee native plants:

  • Cardinal Flower (Lobelia cardinalis) — Red blooms July-September
  • Trumpet Vine (Campsis radicans) — Orange trumpets June-September
  • Bee Balm (Monarda didyma) — Pink/red June-August
  • Columbine (Aquilegia canadensis) — Red/yellow April-May
  • Red Buckeye (Aesculus pavia) — Red spikes April-May
  • Native Honeysuckle (Lonicera sempervirens) — Coral blooms April-July

🎨 Tennessee-Specific Color Strategy

Since Tennessee's state flower is the purple/blue iris, consider this feeder placement strategy:

  • Primary feeders: Red (proven hummingbird attractor)
  • Secondary feeders: Purple/blue (Iris color harmony, garden aesthetic)
  • Flower clusters: Plant red cardinal flower + purple iris together
