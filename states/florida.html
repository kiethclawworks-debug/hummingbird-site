🌸 Florida's Year-Round Flower Power

Here's what makes Florida different from Tennessee or Virginia:

  • Year-round blooming: Firebush, shrimp plant, lantana never stop in South Florida
  • Consistent nectar: Same 3-sugar blend, all year — no seasonal variation like northern states
  • Continuous feeding: Ruby-throats don't need to migrate out of South Florida — food is always here
💡 Why Florida Hummingbirds Stay Year-Round: In South Florida, coral honeysuckle blooms in January. Firebush blooms in December. The 3-sugar nectar these flowers produce is available 365 days a year. Hummingbirds that find a good territory here don't need to leave.

🌿 Florida's Best Hummingbird Flowers

Skip the orange blossoms (pretty but useless for hummers). Plant these:

Statewide Winners:

  • Coral Honeysuckle (Lonicera sempervirens) — THE Florida hummingbird flower. Blooms March-November (year-round in South). Statewide.
  • Firebush (Hamelia patens) — Red/orange blooms year-round in South, April-November in North. Attracts hummers constantly.
  • Shrimp Plant (Justicia brandegeeana) — Not native but naturalized. Pink/bronze blooms year-round in South. Hummers LOVE it.

North & Central Florida:

  • Cardinal Flower (Lobelia cardinalis) — Red July-October, moist areas
  • Red Buckeye (Aesculus pavia) — Red March-April
  • Native Azaleas — Spring bloom, understory
💡 Florida Gardening Secret: Firebush + Coral Honeysuckle + Shrimp Plant = hummingbird paradise in South Florida. All bloom continuously. Add a water mister and you'll have birds all year.
